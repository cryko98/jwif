The Lore

The legend of Jimmy

Every OG has an origin story. Jimmy's has a ball of yarn in it.

  1. I

    The Boards

    Long before memecoins, before charts, before anyone said "wagmi", there was Jimmy. A gorilla with a smirk that said "I know something you don't." He showed up in every thread on the old 4chan boards. He never posted a word. He didn't need to. Anons screenshotted him, reposted him, put him in places he had no business being. Jimmy was everywhere.

  2. II

    The Long Winter

    But the internet moves fast. Threads 404'd. New memes took the front page. Jimmy got archived — left on a dusty server in a forgotten folder, next to rage comics and dial-up sounds. It was cold in the archive. Really cold. And Jimmy, through all of it, had nothing on his head.

  3. III

    The Hat

    In the dark, Jimmy found a ball of salmon-coloured yarn some anon had left behind. He had no instructions. He had big hands. He had time. One stitch per red candle, one row per bear market — and slowly, a hat. Chunky. Slightly lopsided. Completely his. He put it on, and for the first time in years, the smirk came back.

  4. IV

    The Return

    Jimmy didn't come back to the old boards. He walked straight onto Solana — fast, cheap, warm — and launched himself on pump.fun. No presale. No VC table. Same bonding curve for every anon. Just a gorilla, a hat, and a smirk that still knows something you don't.

"Memes come and go. The hat stays on." — Jimmy, probably
